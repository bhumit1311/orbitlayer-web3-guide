Layer 2 overview

Why Ethereum needed Arbitrum

Ethereum got crowded

Ethereum mainnet is secure and decentralized, but many users competing for block space can make fees expensive and confirmations slower.

Arbitrum moves work off mainnet

Arbitrum processes many transactions on Layer 2, groups them together, and posts compressed data back to Ethereum.

Users get lower cost

A real-world benefit is cheaper swaps, transfers, games, and app interactions compared with doing every step directly on Ethereum mainnet.
